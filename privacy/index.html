Privacy
policy.

pocket.type collects no data about you. No account, no ads, no analytics, and it works without the internet.

Last updated 30 October 2026

What stays on your phone

Everything you type, choose, save and export stays on your device. The tools, fonts and effects are all inside the app.

Nothing is sent to me or to anyone else.

Photos

When you export, the app asks permission to add the clip or image to your photo library. This is add only.

The app cannot see or read the photos and videos you already have.

What I do not do

I do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage.

I do not track you across other apps or websites. I do not use third party analytics, advertising or crash reporting.

Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give me anonymous crash and usage reports.

I cannot tell who they come from.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, I will update this page and the date above before the new version is released.
