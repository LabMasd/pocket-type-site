Where do my exports go?

Videos and images are saved to your camera roll. Save to Files puts the file wherever you choose.

Can I make a clip loop?

Yes. Open Export and turn on Make it loop. The clip ends exactly where it began.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, no sign in and nothing to sync.

Where are my saved looks?

On your phone, in the app. They are not uploaded anywhere.
